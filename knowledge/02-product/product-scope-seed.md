# Product scope seed (hypothesis)
Constraints already decided: see GOV-003 (non-writers included; AI from full
drafting to reformatting; storefront + PDF first; Amazon/Gumroad later).
- M1 Ebook creation: projects, audience/goal/tone, AI outline, section
  writing, editing + version history, sources, quality checks, covers,
  PDF first (EPUB evaluated), retries, preview.
- M2 Publishing/storefront: listing, previews, checkout, pricing, orders,
  delivery, receipts, refunds, access control.
- M3 Marketing: ad copy, creative briefs, landing pages, email sequences;
  manual approval before any paid ad action.
- M4 Niche research: pain points, topics, competition, demand hypotheses.
- M5 Analytics: revenue, conversion, CAC, ad spend, refunds, margins, usage.
- M6 SaaS admin: auth, tenants, RBAC, plans, credits, metering, billing, audit.
First MVP hypothesis: project creation, outline/chapter generation,
editing, professional PDF, basic quality review, simple storefront and
secure delivery. Not every module is in the MVP.
Content model: Book > Chapter > Section > Content block, with revisions and
source metadata; regenerate one section without overwriting the book.
